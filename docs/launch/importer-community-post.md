## Title

The Section 122 surcharge ends today — free calendar of every US tariff
effective date and comment deadline

## Body

If you import into the US you've spent the year being surprised by
effective dates: IEEPA struck down in February, the 10% Section 122
surcharge sunsetting today, de minimis gone, new forced-labor Section 301
investigations against 60 countries since June.

I built a free calendar you can subscribe to in Google/Outlook/Apple that
carries every upcoming effective date, comment deadline, and hearing from
the Federal Register's trade docket:

https://tariff.watch/calendar.ics

Behind it is a daily changelog (https://tariff.watch/?ref=reddit) where
every entry links the actual government document — no paraphrased news,
no paywall on the human surfaces. Sources are checked four times a day.

Not legal advice; every entry links the primary source so your broker can
verify in one click. Built by one person — if a date looks wrong, tell me
and it gets fixed against the source.
